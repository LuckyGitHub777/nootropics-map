# UX Principles

## Experience goal

Museum calm + laboratory rigor.

## Rules

1. **One obvious action.** Search or explore.
2. **Progressive disclosure.** Simple → Evidence → Sources.
3. **Uncertainty is visible.** Never bury it in footnotes.
4. **Plain language first.** Technical language is available, not mandatory.
5. **No gamification of risky interventions.** No winner podiums, streaks, or purchase-style calls to action.
6. **Luxury by restraint.** Typography, spacing, motion, and clarity do the work.
7. **Accessible by default.** Keyboard navigation, focus states, reduced-motion support, semantic HTML, readable contrast.
8. **Trust on demand.** Every record exposes provenance, review date, limitations, and evidence state.

## Signature interaction

The community map shows how the published 2022 chart works: number of ratings against the reported chance of a life-changing rating, on logarithmic axes, with the published regions and a few cited items drawn with their model intervals. It is an original diagram, not a reproduction of either version of the chart.

The evidence check then shows evidence maturity and confidence for each reviewed claim as two separate ordinal measures, without implying effect size. A quantitative Community Signal layer must not ship until it has its own source-backed provenance.
