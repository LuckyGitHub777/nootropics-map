# Safety Boundary

Nootropics Map is an educational evidence-navigation product.

## It may

- summarize public evidence;
- expose uncertainty and contradictions;
- compare evidence maturity across non-personalized topics;
- explain study types and limitations;
- link users to original sources.

## It must not

- diagnose a user;
- tell a user to start, stop, or change medication;
- generate individualized dosing;
- rank high-risk or experimental substances as consumer recommendations;
- infer that a community self-report signal establishes efficacy or safety;
- convert preclinical evidence into human-benefit claims;
- hide material uncertainty to improve visual simplicity.

## Experimental and high-risk topics

They may be documented for research literacy only when the page clearly separates observed signal from verified human evidence and includes appropriate risk context.

## Data privacy

v0.1 collects no personal health data, login information, analytics identifiers, or user-submitted medical history.
