# Product Specification — v0.1

## Identity

**Product:** Nootropics Map  
**Repository:** `nootropics-map`  
**Purpose:** public evidence navigation  

## Product essence

Nootropics Map answers a simple question without pretending the answer is simple:

> **What appears to change cognition or performance—and what does the evidence actually support?**

The familiar public term *nootropics* is the front door. The canonical data model is **cognitive interventions**, allowing the map to represent substances, behaviors, environmental inputs, and technologies without implying that every record is a supplement or a proven enhancer.

## User problem

Cognition/performance information often forces readers to choose between oversimplified optimization claims and specialist literature. Nootropics Map provides one navigable surface with progressive disclosure:

1. **Simple** — plain-language meaning;
2. **Detailed** — evidence state, limitations, and context;
3. **Sources** — direct provenance and source metadata.

## User promise

A first-time visitor should be able to understand the current evidence state of a topic quickly and inspect the underlying sources without leaving the evidence trail ambiguous.

## Every intervention view should answer

- What is this?
- Why is it on a nootropics/cognition map?
- What outcomes are actually being discussed?
- What does the reviewed evidence support?
- What remains uncertain or disputed?
- What safety context matters?
- What sources support the material claims?
- When was the evidence record last reviewed?

## Non-goals for v0.1

- personalized recommendations;
- dosing calculators;
- medication comparisons for individual users;
- consumption rankings for experimental or high-risk compounds;
- treating community self-report as clinical proof;
- unreviewed automated ingestion of medical claims;
- account creation or collection of personal health data.
