What stands out

Five things that stand out

  1. Behaviors appear alongside compounds

    Weightlifting, sleep, meditation and Doing Math appear in the same landscape as supplements and other interventions.

  2. Popularity and reported impact are different

    Items with many ratings appear at very different levels of reported life-changing impact. Being widely rated does not place an item high on the chart.

  3. Common does not mean life-changing

    Caffeine, creatine, theanine and omega-3 each draw many ratings, yet sit lower for reported life-changing impact than weightlifting, sleep or meditation.

  4. The chart summarizes reported experience

    Positions reflect how people rated their own experience. They do not establish efficacy, causation or safety.

  5. Scientific evidence answers a different question

    Reviewed scientific evidence for selected items appears below, separate from the chart and from how people rated their experience.
