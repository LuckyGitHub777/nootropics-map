What stands out

Seven things that stand out

  1. 2022 chart

    Behaviors sit among the compounds

    Weightlifting, trying to get more sleep and meditation all place in the top ten of 125 items for life-changing probability.

  2. 2022 chart

    Weightlifting ranks third

    Third of 125 items at 0.121, above every supplement and every other behavior in the table.

  3. 2022 chart

    Caffeine: most rated, rarely life-changing

    2,553 ratings, more than any other item, with a life-changing probability of 0.030.

  4. 2022 chart

    Familiar supplements sit low

    Creatine, theanine and omega-3 each drew 956 to 1,322 ratings and all fall in the common, rarely life-changing region.

  5. 2022 chart

    Popularity barely tracks impact

    Across the 116 plotted items, rating count and life-changing probability have a rank correlation of only ρ ≈ 0.14.

  6. 2022 chart

    Sparse points are uncertain

    Items with fewer than 50 ratings carry intervals about four times wider than items with 500 or more.

  7. Later image

    A later image includes “Doing Math”

    “Doing Math” is absent from the published 2022 dataset but appears prominently in a separate later image. The later image’s underlying data is unavailable, so no numerical comparison is made.
