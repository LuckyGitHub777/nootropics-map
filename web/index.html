WHY TRUST THIS?

Trust the trace, not the aesthetic.

The interface is presentation. Structured evidence records are the system of record.

Source-first

Material claims map to structured source IDs.

Qualified

Claim status and limitations are displayed independently.

Versionable

Evidence changes are designed to be reviewed as Git diffs.

Bounded

No personalized diagnosis, treatment, dosing, or medication-change instructions.

METHOD

Separate reputation, self-report, and evidence.

v0.1 uses intentionally coarse evidence bands. “Nootropics” is the public front door; inclusion is not proof of enhancement, safety, or a recommendation.
