Two source states

Published 2022 analysis. 36,163 ratings from 2,802 people; a public result table of 125 items, of which the published code plots the 116 with more than 10 new ratings. Every number on this page comes from this state.

Later supplied image. A larger version of the chart image also circulates. The later image includes labels such as Doing Math that are absent from the 2022 result table, and it draws its y-axis differently. Its underlying dataset has not been published, so none of the 2022 totals or positions are assigned to it.

Two questions

Signal is not evidence. Evidence is not effect size.

The community chart and the reviewed records are most useful side by side, as long as neither is read as the other.

Different datasets and different questions can produce different-looking signals; they should not be treated as contradictory unless the claims actually match. Creatine rarely felt life-changing to raters asked about cognition, while the reviewed claim is about strength, power and repeated high-intensity effort. Those are different questions, not a disagreement.
